Overview

Purpose

To promote world-class whitewater rafting excursions in the Appalachian Mountains, inspiring adventurers to book guided single-day or multi-day river trips.

Audience

Outdoor enthusiasts, college students on break, and thrill-seekers along the East Coast looking to experience historic river gorges. Visitors will access this site via mobile devices while planning road trips, as well as desktop computers at home.
